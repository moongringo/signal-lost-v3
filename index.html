Detection Mechanics
Active Detection
Patrol Drone: Visual LOS scan every 1.2s. Spots moving targets in open terrain. Stealth reduces range by 40%.
Trail Cam: Motion trigger + snapshot. Ignores crouch-walk below 0.8 m/s. Sends alert to Spotter instantly.
Signal Sweep: Hacker ability. Pings all active electronics in 50m. Reveals hidden devices for 4s.
Passive Detection
Listening Post: Always-on audio. Footsteps, gunfire, and vehicle engines detected in 15m. No LOS required.
Proximity Mine: Pressure plate. Triggers on any entity within 3m after 2s arm delay. No warning.
Sniper Drone: Hidden until firing. Muzzle flash reveals position for 3s. Then re-conceals.
After Detection
What Happens After Detection
1. Track — Position updated on squad minimap every 2s for 10s.
2. Mark — Target highlighted with red diamond. +15% damage from squad.
3. Engage — Sniper Drone auto-fires. Mines trigger. Claymores detonate.
4. Countermeasure — Jammer blocks signal. Decoy redirects lock. EMP kills device.
5. Alert Team — All squad members notified via HUD ping and audio cue.
Counterplay
Counterplay Options
Fog of War: Dense smoke, weather, and night reduce drone vision range by 60%. Thermal upgrades bypass this.
Kill the Drone: Small arms, 2-3 hits. Drops scrap for Engineer. Disables all linked spotting for 15s.
Signal Jamming: Jammer blocks control in 20m. Hacker can hijack instead with 4s channel.
Ghost Mode: Crouch-walk below 0.8 m/s. No drone spot. Thermal still sees you.
